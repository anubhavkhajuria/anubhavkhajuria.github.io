01 / Research

Performance is a
systems problem.

My PhD research at Washington State University brings together deep learning compilers, hardware-software co-design, and 2.5D and 3D chiplet-based systems.

I build compiler optimizations for resource-constrained environments such as Edge AI and specialized sparse accelerators. Using infrastructures like MLIR and LLVM, I work across the stack—from intermediate representations and optimization passes to target-specific code generation.

I’m interested in closing the gap between high-level abstractions and the hardware that executes them, from GPU programming to accelerator and chiplet-based systems.
