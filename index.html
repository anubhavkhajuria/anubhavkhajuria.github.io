01 / Research

Research

My PhD research at Washington State University focuses on 2.5D and 3D chiplet-based systems and hardware-software co-design.

My compiler work develops MLIR/LLVM pass pipelines that map sparse tensor workloads to RISC-V vector extensions for Edge AI accelerators.

Across compilers, GPUs, and chiplet packages, I study how computation and data movement shape performance.
